3D ICE: Explore Antarctica and Greenland in 3D

Public Research

Explore Antarctica from every angle. Rotate, zoom, and peel back the ice to uncover a hidden world in interactive 3D.

Everything you see is built from published measurements and models: maps of the ice and the bedrock beneath it, ice speeds measured from satellites, ocean models, projections from eight ice-sheet models, and a published calculation of how the land rebounds. Tap the i beside a layer for its source.

New here? Take the guided tour

Nine short stops, from the hidden land beneath the ice to its future and a world without it. It takes about six minutes.
